############################
Account Payment Nacha Module
############################

The *Account Payment Nacha Module* creates ACH files in the Nacha format for
the payments processed with a journal using the *Nacha (ACH)* method.

The *Chase* profile enforces the rules of the Chase for Business ACH File
Upload specifications (May 2024): no balanced file, credits and debits in
separate files, the funding account in the company discretionary data, a
mandatory identification number, no pre-note and an effective date after the
creation date.
