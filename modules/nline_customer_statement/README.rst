################################
N-Line Customer Statement Module
################################

The *N-Line Customer Statement Module* prints the full customer statement
QuickBooks users know, instead of the past-due letter only:

- the balance forward at the start of the period;
- every posted invoice, credit memo and payment of the period on the
  receivable accounts, with its due date and the running balance;
- the aging of the open amount at the statement date (current, 1-30, 31-60,
  61-90 and over 90 days past due) and the amount due.

*Customer Statements* (Accounting > Reporting, or *Print* on customers) prints
the statements of the chosen customers, or of every customer with activity in
the period or an open balance. The report can be printed or sent by email from
the client.
