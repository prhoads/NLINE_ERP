########################
N-Line Change Log Module
########################

The *N-Line Change Log Module* keeps a field-level change log of the
accounting and master records with the standard record history of Tryton:
every saved version of the records is kept (from the activation of the
module on).

Tracked models: account moves and lines, accounts, journals, invoices and
their lines and taxes, payment terms, payments and payment groups, bank
statements and lines, parties and addresses, products, productions and BOMs,
purchases, sales and their lines (those of activated modules).

The *Change Log* (Accounting > Reporting, or *Relate* on an invoice, a move,
a payment, a party or a product) lists, for a record or for all the records
of a model changed in a period, who created, changed or deleted what and
when, with the old and the new value of each changed field.
